On the AU calendar Google Calendar never mixes a subscribed-by-URL calendar (like mitstudie.au.dk's feed) into "primary" — it stays a separate calendar. The card now looks up every calendar you've imported that way and merges its events in, tagged AU so you can tell them apart from your own. That's what was missing before, not a refresh problem.
On the LinkedIn jobs card It searches your inbox for mail from jobalerts-noreply@linkedin.com — LinkedIn's real job-alert sender. You don't have any yet (I checked), so it'll sit empty until you turn on email alerts for a saved search — student/working-student, developer, Aarhus — on LinkedIn itself; once those start arriving, this card picks them up automatically, no changes needed here.

ZipRecruiter stayed out: it's the only job-search connector available, but it only covers the US and Canada — a live test against "developer, Aarhus, Denmark" came back with irrelevant US listings. There's no Danish job board connector to fall back on either.

On your phone Open this page in Safari on your iPhone, tap the Share icon, then Add to Home Screen. It'll sit on your home screen with its own icon and open full-screen, no browser bar — as close to a native app as a page like this gets. It still needs an internet connection to refresh; there's no offline mode.
